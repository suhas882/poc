Data-tier simulator

Proposed 24-week milestones

  1. M1, weeks 1–4: install, onboarding, synthetic sandbox and baseline.
  2. M2, weeks 5–13: replay closed synthetic requests, tune retrieval and shadow-test.
  3. M3, weeks 14–19: measure KPIs, survey staff and review accessibility/equity.
  4. Closeout, weeks 20–24: summary, report and showcase, subject to partner approval.

02

AI and staff-control guardrails

SIMULATED POLICY VIEW
  • Staff review and edit each search plan before running it.
  • Search is read-only. No records are released, redacted, or marked exempt by the tool.
  • No model call, city repository, portal, or source permission is connected.
  • Logs are exportable. Synthetic logs are drafts, not an official diligent-search record.
  • Do not enter real requester or city data in this demo.
